# What the licence covers

This licence covers everything in this repository that is this project's own
work: the source code, the documentation, and the pictures, recordings and
drawings that go with them. "The Software" in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) means all
of that.

Two kinds of thing here carry somebody else's rights as well, and reusing
them means carrying those too.

`shell/assets/wordmark.txt` takes its letterforms from Omarchy's `logo.txt`,
Copyright (c) David Heinemeier Hansson, MIT, whose licence travels beside it
as `shell/assets/LICENSE.omarchy`.

The screenshots and recordings under `docs/` show what was on the screen, and
what was on the screen is not all this project's: game titles, cover art,
album art and the interfaces of other programs appear in them. The licence
here covers the part that is this project's own, the launcher's drawing and
the composition of the picture. It does not, and cannot, grant anything over
the other material in the frame, whose rights stay with whoever holds them.
Treat those images as illustrations of this software rather than as assets to
reuse. `THIRD-PARTY.md` names the programs and services involved.
