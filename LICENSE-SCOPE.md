# What the licence covers

This licence covers everything in this repository: the source code, the
documentation, and the pictures, recordings and drawings that go with it.
"The Software" in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) means all of it.

Two of those pieces carry somebody else's copyright as well as this one, and
reusing them means carrying that notice too. `shell/assets/wordmark.txt`
takes its letterforms from Omarchy's `logo.txt`, Copyright (c) David
Heinemeier Hansson, MIT, whose licence travels beside it as
`shell/assets/LICENSE.omarchy`. The screenshots and recordings under `docs/`
show the interfaces of other programs, each under its own terms.
`THIRD-PARTY.md` names every one of them.
