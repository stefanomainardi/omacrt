Flyback · the compositor
Retrace is the dark cut of the beam coming back between two lines — four bars standing still, the return moving down and to the left. Flyback is the same beam seen as its waveform — the ramp stepping down and to the right, and the stroke where it collapses. Four bars, eight thick, four apart, a 44 unit grid: one alphabet, two words. The compositor is drawn in the phosphor green it reports its own latency in; the product keeps paper.
Rules. The mark's height is twice the wordmark's cap height, and the second line's baseline sits on the bottom edge of the mark's last bar, so the two blocks bottom out together. The space between them is the mark's own gap, 4 units, scaled. Never set the word in anything but JetBrains Mono 800, and never letterspace it apart. Where the name has to appear without the mark, the word alone is fine; the mark alone is fine everywhere.
