Direction C - leading
The ramp, and the stroke back
The other meaning of the name. A television's horizontal deflection is a sawtooth: the current ramps across the screen and then collapses back in almost no time at all - that collapse is the flyback, and the transformer named after it is the part that dies when the line rate is wrong. So the mark is the waveform: four bars stepping out on the same 8-thick, 4-gap rhythm as Retrace, and one full-height stroke where the ramp resets.
