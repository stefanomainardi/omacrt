# Code of conduct

This is one person's television, given away because it turned out well. The
rules here are short because the project is small.

**Be decent.** Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments about
people are not welcome, in issues, in pull requests, or in discussions. Neither
is anything you would not say to somebody standing in your living room looking
at your tube.

**Argue about the work, not the person.** Strong disagreement about a design,
a timing, a modeline or a line of Rust is the point of the thing. Disagreement
about somebody's character is not.

**Assume the other person has a television too.** Most of what looks like a
wrong answer here is a different set of hardware: another DAC, another kernel,
another set that locks at a slightly different rate. Ask what they are running
before deciding they are wrong.

If somebody is making the project a worse place to be, write to the address on
[stefanomainardi.com](https://www.stefanomainardi.com). Reports stay between
you and the maintainer. Anything from a comment left alone to a block,
depending on what happened.
